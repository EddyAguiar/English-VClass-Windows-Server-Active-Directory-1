📘 1. Core Concepts — Identity in a Domain

Quatro ideias sustentam o Day 1. Responda cada pergunta e veja a explicação.

🏢 Local vs Domain AccountA2–B1
❓ Where does a domain account live?
✅ Uma local account existe em uma única máquina. Uma domain account vive no banco central do Domain Controller (DC) e funciona em todos os PCs ingressados no domínio.
Example: A local account exists on one machine. A domain account is stored on the Domain Controller and works on every domain-joined PC.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 local account → conta local 🔊 domain account → conta de domínio 🔊 Domain Controller → controlador de domínio
📁 Organizational Unit (OU)A2–B1
❓ What is an OU used for?
✅ Uma OU é como uma pasta que organiza usuários e computadores — e, amanhã (Day 2), é onde você aplica GPOs. Permissões não ficam na OU.
Example: Carla is in the Finance OU, so she receives the Finance policies automatically.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 Organizational Unit → unidade organizacional 🔊 object → objeto 🔊 policy → política
🔐 Security GroupB1
❓ Where should permissions live?
✅ Conceda a permissão ao grupo e depois adicione ou remova pessoas dele. Assim você não edita a permissão da pasta para cada usuário.
Example: I add Carla to SG-Finance-Share-Read because the group already has read permission on the folder.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 security group → grupo de segurança 🔊 permission → permissão 🔊 membership → associação / participação
🎫 Login TokenB1–B2
❓ Carla was added to a group while she was logged in, but still gets Access Denied. Why?
✅ As associações de grupo são gravadas no token no momento do login. Depois de mudar o grupo, o usuário precisa fazer sign out / sign in (ou reiniciar).
Example: Please sign out and sign back in so your token refreshes.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 token → token de acesso 🔊 sign out → encerrar sessão 🔊 sign back in → entrar novamente

💬 4. Useful Expressions — Describe & Document

Frases prontas para reportar um problema e escrever notas de ticket claras (Symptom + Cause + Action).

🚨 Describing the SymptomA2–B1
Access: She gets Access Denied. · He can't open the folder.
Login: The user can't log in. · The account is locked out.
Password: The password has expired. · The password must be reset.
📎 Cause, Action & ResultB1–B2
Cause: The root cause is... · It happens because... · Her token was outdated.
Action: I reset the password. · I added her to the group. · I asked her to sign out and back in.
Result: As a result, she can access the folder. · The issue is resolved.
Follow-up: Please confirm if it works. · I'll monitor the ticket.
💡 Fórmula da nota de ticket:
Symptom (what the user sees) + Cause (why it happened) + Action (what you did) + Result (current status).

📝 5. Model Answers — Interview Questions

Respostas completas para perguntas comuns de entrevista de suporte de TI.

Q: What is Active Directory and why do companies use it?
Answer: Active Directory is a central directory that stores users, computers and groups. Companies use it because IT can manage identity and access from one place. In addition, it saves time since nobody has to configure each computer separately.
Q: How do you onboard a new employee?
Answer: First, I create the user with a temporary password and force a change at the next logon. Then I place the user in the correct OU and add them to the right security groups.
Q: How do you offboard an employee?
Answer: I disable the account, reset the password and move it to a Disabled Users OU. I don't delete it immediately because the audit trail and mailbox may still be needed. Finally, I delete it after the retention period.
Q: A user says "Access Denied" on a shared folder. What do you check?
Answer: First, I ask the user to sign out and back in to refresh the token. Then I verify the group membership and the Share and NTFS permissions. Finally, I look for an explicit Deny entry.

🎧 7. Listening Practice — A Day at the Helpdesk with Alex

Ouça o texto e responda às perguntas. Alex usa Symptom + Cause + Action com linking words em um contexto real de suporte.

Hi, my name is Alex and I work as an IT support specialist. Today a new employee called Carla starts in Finance. First, I create her user account in Active Directory with a temporary password, so she must change it at her first logon. Then I place the account in the Finance OU because the OU organizes users and applies policies. Next, I add her to the security group SG-Finance-Share-Read, since permissions should live on groups, not on individual users. In the afternoon, Carla says she gets Access Denied on the Finance folder. However, I know she was already logged in when I added her to the group. Therefore, I ask her to sign out and sign back in. After that, I run whoami /groups to check her token. Although the group is now there, the problem continues, so I check the Share and NTFS permissions. Finally, I find an explicit Deny entry and remove it. As a result, Carla can open the folder. In addition, I write a clear note in the ticket with the cause and the solution.
Olá, meu nome é Alex e trabalho como especialista em suporte de TI. Hoje uma nova funcionária chamada Carla começa em Finance. Primeiro, crio a conta de usuário dela no Active Directory com uma senha temporária, então ela precisa trocá-la no primeiro logon. Depois coloco a conta na OU Finance porque a OU organiza usuários e aplica políticas. Em seguida, adiciono-a ao grupo de segurança SG-Finance-Share-Read, já que as permissões devem ficar nos grupos, não nos usuários individuais. À tarde, Carla diz que recebe Access Denied na pasta Finance. No entanto, sei que ela já estava logada quando a adicionei ao grupo. Portanto, peço que ela saia e entre novamente. Depois disso, executo whoami /groups para verificar o token dela. Embora o grupo agora esteja lá, o problema continua, então verifico as permissões de Share e NTFS. Finalmente, encontro uma entrada de Deny explícito e a removo. Como resultado, Carla consegue abrir a pasta. Além disso, escrevo uma nota clara no ticket com a causa e a solução.

Question 1

What is the difference between a local account and a domain account?

Model: A local account exists on one computer only. A domain account is stored on the Domain Controller, so it works on every domain-joined PC. As a result, IT manages access from one place.

Question 2
